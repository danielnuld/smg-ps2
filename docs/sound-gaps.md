## BGM / ME behaviour (read by hand from the list at the end)

- ME_* music effects: ported (engine/src/me.c, Petari's RhythmLib): MR::startSystemME plays while the music is a rhythm BGM (hasME: its .rhy from tools/rhythm.py gives the beat callbacks and the chord / scale).
- Sequenced BGMs play as tools/bgmseq.py renders (SEQ_*): one stream at a time (a sub BGM takes the stream, the stage's place kept), no cross fade, one volume for the stage and sub BGM (audio_move_volume: moveVolumeStageBGM / SubBGM), no setSoundVolumeSetting.
- Audited 2026-11-20, every BGM / ME id of the list at the end against engine/src: all named. The bosses' startBossBGM / stopStageBGM / startAfterBossBGM / setStageBGMState, the volume moves (the star's appear demo, TalkBalloon, NoteFairy, MechaKoopaPartsHead, the pinch BGM, AudEffectDirector), ChangeBgmCube, BgmProhibitArea, the dome's selector states, the demo sheets' BGM (DemoSoundKeeper), the picture book's STM_PROLOGUE_01 and Bowser's grand star fanfares (PowerStar::exeAppearDemoKoopa) are in. Not checked call by call: Dodoryu, Koopa, Polta, SkeletalFishBoss, TripodBoss, IceMerameraKing, KoopaJrShip, Tamakoro (their counts match), the timing of each call, and anything by ear.
