## BGM / ME behaviour (read by hand from the list at the end)

- ME_* music effects: MR::startSystemME plays only with a chord table (hasME); the port has no BGM sequencer, so every ME call takes its SE fallback or nothing (FlipPanel, rally combos, AstroDome select).
- Sequenced BGMs play as tools/bgmseq.py renders (SEQ_*): one stream at a time, no cross fade, no moveVolumeStageBGM / moveVolumeSubBGM (PowerStar's appear demo, NoteFairy), no setSoundVolumeSetting.
- Boss BGMs (MR::startBossBGM: MBGM_BOSS_*), startAfterBossBGM (MBGM_STAR_EXIST), event BGMs (races, chases), the miss / game over sub BGMs and the clear fanfare need the BGM ids beyond the scenario table.
- Demos: the star return / Rosetta demos' BGM events, BGM fade-in after demos, ChangeBgmCube BGM changes.
